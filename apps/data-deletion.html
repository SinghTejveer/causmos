Deleting your data

MarginsReads app · last updated 29 October 2026

The app keeps very little: if you asked a ForkableX post for its links, your Instagram username, the comment's ID and which post you asked about (for up to 7 days), and the IDs of comments and messages it has already answered. See the privacy policy.

To have it deleted, either:

  1. send the word DELETE as an Instagram message to the account you messaged, or
  2. email tejveer02singh@gmail.com with your Instagram username.

Everything held about you is deleted within 30 days, usually the same day, and you get a reply to confirm it.

You can also remove the conversation from your own Instagram inbox at any time; that deletes it on your side only.
