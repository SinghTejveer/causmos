Privacy Policy

MarginsReads app · last updated 29 October 2026

"MarginsReads" is a private Meta app run by Tejveer Singh (United Kingdom). It publishes posts for three of his own accounts, and answers people who ask for links on one of them:

  • Margins Reads (Instagram @margins.reads and its Facebook Page): daily book posts;
  • The Causmos (Instagram @thecausmos and its Facebook Page): short explainer reels;
  • ForkableX (Instagram @forkablex and its Facebook Page): posts about free, open-source software.

It is not offered to anyone else, and no one else logs in to it.

What it reads, and why

  • Comments on our own posts. On ForkableX posts the app reads comments to find ones that ask for the links by writing "STACK". It reads the comment text, the commenter's Instagram username and the comment's ID.
  • Messages people send us. When someone who asked for the links replies to our message, the app reads that reply so it can answer it.
  • Whether you follow the account. When you reply, the app asks Instagram whether you follow @forkablex, and uses the answer only to decide which reply to send.

It does not read anything else about you, and it never reads messages in conversations you did not start with us.

What it sends

One private reply to a "STACK" comment, a public "Check your DMs ✓" under it, and then either the list of links or a short reminder to follow, in reply to your messages. It sends nothing you did not ask for, and no marketing.

What it keeps, and for how long

  • Your Instagram username, the comment ID and which post you asked about, for up to 7 days or until the links are sent, whichever is first;
  • the IDs of comments and messages already answered (not their text), so nobody is answered twice; message IDs are kept for the 500 most recent only.

All of it is stored on one private server in the United Kingdom. Nothing is sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to build profiles.

Deleting your data

Email tejveer02singh@gmail.com with your Instagram username, or send the word DELETE to the account you messaged, and anything held about you is deleted within 30 days (usually the same day). See data deletion.

Meta and Instagram

The app uses Meta's Instagram and Facebook APIs and follows the Meta Platform Terms. Meta's own handling of your data is described in the Instagram Privacy Policy.
